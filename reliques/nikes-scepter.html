Nike's Scepter

Rareté : SSR / UR

Faction : Sanctuary

Tags : Enhancement, Back Only

Restriction : Back Row

Description

A relic held in Athena's possession throughout history. What most people fail to realize is that it is actually an alternative form of Athena's Goddess of Victory statue. Wielding her celestial staff, Athena inspires the Saints and leads them to victory.

Effet principal

At the start of the battle, Nike's Scepter grants Light of Victory to all allies, increasing their ATK by 50%. This effect decays over 10 seconds and is not cumulative with identical artifacts.

Remarques stratégiques

Nike's Scepter est une relique majeure de support offensif, idéale pour les compositions burst ou opening damage. Elle excelle dans les équipes Sanctuary ou Athena-centric, mais reste extrêmement forte en universel Back Row.
